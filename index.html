A Letter
— for you

My Dearest

I am writing this because some feelings deserve paper — even when the world is loud. You make my days softer, my laughter fuller, and my plans brighter.

With you, the small things — a shared coffee, a quiet walk, a silly joke — become everything I want to hold onto. I love the way you make ordinary moments feel like home.

Thank you for being patient, kind, and wonderfully you. I carry you with me, always.

Always,
— Yours
